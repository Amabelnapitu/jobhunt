How applying works
  1. Each morning the scraper ranks new jobs, picks today's top 20 you haven't applied to, and tailors your CV for each.
  2. Open a job: check the tailored CV (and its one-page PDF), see why each change was made, fill in what the posting asks for, then press Apply. That is your approval. No time today? Press Save for later; it stays under Saved & earlier even if it drops out of the top 20.
  3. The worker on your computer (an hourly Claude task in the desktop app, using your signed-in browser) submits approved applications with the PDF attached and marks them Applied. It never invents an answer: a question it can't answer from your CV, About me or your answers comes back here as Needs you. Logins, verification codes and CAPTCHAs also come back to you.

How applying works
  1. Each morning the scraper ranks new jobs and picks today's top 20 you haven't applied to.
  2. Open a job, fill in what it asks for, tick the documents you have, then press Ready to apply.
  3. In the Claude app, say "apply to my ready jobs". Claude fills each application in your browser, shows you the filled form, and submits only after your OK. Nothing is invented: anything missing comes back to you as a question.
